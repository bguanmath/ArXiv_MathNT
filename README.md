<details><summary>Abstract</summary><p>We use a gluing procedure introduced by Ginzburg to describe the relative Langlands dual of the hyperspherical Hamiltonian $(\mathrm{GL}_n \times \mathrm{GL}_m)$-variety $T^*(\mathrm{Hom}(\mathbb{C}^m,\mathbb{C}^n))$, and in particular the Rankin-Selberg case $m=n$. > **Comment:** 37 pages

<details><summary>Abstract</summary><p>Let $p$ be an odd prime, and let $V_1$ and $V_2$ be symplectic self-dual $p$-adic de Rham representations of $G_{\mathbb{Q}}$ that are pure (of weight $-1$) and satisfy the Panchishkin condition at $p$. > **Comment:** This is a revised and extended version of a published article

<details><summary>Abstract</summary><p>Let $θ(x)=(x-1)/3$ if $x\ge1$ and $θ(x)=2x/(1-x)$ if $0\le x<1$. We conjecture that the $θ$-orbit of every nonnegative rational number ends in $0$. > **Comment:** 20 pages, 1 figure, big update to include resolution of 2-large versus large

<details><summary>Abstract</summary><p>A set $S$ of positive integers is $r$-large if every $r$-coloring of $\mathbb{N}$ contains arbitrarily long monochromatic arithmetic progressions with common difference in $S$.
