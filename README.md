<details><summary>Abstract</summary><p>We introduce a concept of counital comodule algebra $(B,δ_B,ε_B)$ over a Hopf ${\mathbb K}$-algebra $H$, where $δ_B: B\rightarrow B \otimes H$ and $ε_B: B\rightarrow {\mathbb K}$ are coaction and counit homomorphisms, respectively. ### \[<a href='https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12776v1'>2611.12776</a>\]&nbsp; **Why the non-abelian Gyarmati--Matolcsi--Ruzsa inequality does not generally work: mechanisms and explicit examples**


 &nbsp;&nbsp;|&nbsp;&nbsp;**Date:** 2026-11-09 &nbsp;&nbsp;|&nbsp;&nbsp; **Authors:** Christian Elsholtz &nbsp;&nbsp;|&nbsp;&nbsp; **Category:** math.NT &nbsp;&nbsp;|&nbsp;&nbsp; 

<details><summary>Abstract</summary><p>For finite sets in a commutative semigroup, Gyarmati, Matolcsi and Ruzsa proved $$ |A_1+\dots+A_n|^{n-1}\ \le\ \prod_{i=1}^n|A_1+\dots+A_{i-1}+A_{i+1}+\dots+A_n| . $$ They asked whether in an arbitrary group $$ |A_1\cdots A_n|^{n-1}\ \le\ \prod_{i=1}^n\ \max_{a\in A_i}\,|A_1\cdots A_{i-1}\,a\,A_{i+1}\cdots A_n| , $$ where each omitted set is replaced by the largest slice obtained by fixing one of its elements. Nair, Namayandeh and Zhu proved that this inequality does not hold when $n=4$ and $G=D_4^{14}$, of group order $8^{14}$. We develop its double-coset mechanism and construct counterexamples in many other groups. For $n=4$, the smallest group found has order $518\,400$, namely $A_6^2\times C_2^2$; no absolute minimality is claimed. For $n=6$ there is a witness in a group of order $1728$, for $n=10$ a class-three $3$-group of order $243$ suffices, and for $n=4$ a single class-two $2$-group of order $2^{23}$ works. We also obtain torsion-free examples. The violation ratio $R_n=|A_1\cdots A_n|^{n-1}/\prod_iN_i$ is greater than one exactly when the inequality fails; explicit four-set constructions attain $R_4\approx1.29$ in $A_8^2$, and a larger ratio of about $1.43$ is reported for $A_{10}^2$. For every $n\ge4$, the inequality holds in all direct powers of $G$ precisely when $G$ is abelian. For $n\ge2$ the optimal exponent $θ_n$ is the infimum of $\log(\prod_iN_i)/\log|A_1\cdots A_n|$ over products of size at least two. We obtain $2\leθ_n\le n-1.0768$, and an infinite family in $C_3*C_3$ shows $θ_n\le n-1-δ$ for every $δ<5-2\log_25\approx0.3561$ and all sufficiently large $n$.</p></details>

<details><summary>Abstract</summary><p>This work continues the research of the author on estimates for the number of solutions of algebraic diophantine equations by means of the circle method and develops this line in the geometric direction, towards counting integral points on log Fano varieties. <details><summary>Abstract</summary><p>Given a sequence ${\bf g}: g_0,\dots,g_m$ in a finite group $G$ with $g_0=1_G$, let $\bar{\bf g}:\bar g_0,\dots,\bar g_m$ be the sequence of consecutive quotients of $\bf g$, defined by $\bar g_0=1_G$ and $\bar g_i=g_{i-1}^{-1}g_i$ for $1\le i\le m$.
