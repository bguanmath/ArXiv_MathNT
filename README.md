<details><summary>Abstract</summary><p>We study the Iwasawa $μ$- and $λ$-invariants of quadratic twists of rational elliptic curves at Eisenstein primes $p\geq 3$. ### \[<a href='https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.42080v1'>2610.42080</a>\]&nbsp; **Relative homological algebra of filter modules**


 &nbsp;&nbsp;|&nbsp;&nbsp;**Date:** 2026-10-30 &nbsp;&nbsp;|&nbsp;&nbsp; **Authors:** Fritz Grimpen &nbsp;&nbsp;|&nbsp;&nbsp; **Category:** math.RT &nbsp;&nbsp;|&nbsp;&nbsp; 


> **Comment:** 33 pages, 4 figures. Comments welcome

<details><summary>Abstract</summary><p>Over an arbitrary ordered set we consider the class of filter modules, i.e. indicator modules of filters in the ordered set. We show that the induced exact structure of filter modules, the so-called filter exact structure, which has filter modules as relative projective modules, admits an explicit projectivization by the distributive lattice of filters. This allows the reduction of the projective-homological properties of the induced exact structure to the standard homological algebra of $k{\mathrm{Fil}(Q)}$, the incidence algebra of $k$-representations on the lattice of filters. <details><summary>Abstract</summary><p>Let $G_0$ be a real form of a complex semisimple group acting on a flag manifold $Z$, with maximal compact subgroup $K_0$ and its complexification $K$. ### \[<a href='https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37711v2'>2610.37711</a>\]&nbsp; **The View-Obstruction Problem for Spheres in Dimensions Six and Seven**


 &nbsp;&nbsp;|&nbsp;&nbsp;**Date:** 2026-10-30 &nbsp;&nbsp;|&nbsp;&nbsp; **Authors:** Vineet Kahlon &nbsp;&nbsp;|&nbsp;&nbsp; **Category:** math.NT &nbsp;&nbsp;|&nbsp;&nbsp; 

<details><summary>Abstract</summary><p>Place closed Euclidean balls of radius r at the points of (1/2, 1/2, ..., 1/2) + Z^n. Equivalently, it asks for D_n = sup_v min_{t in R} sum_i ||t v_i - 1/2||{Z}^2 over positive integer vectors v, where ||x||{Z} denotes the distance from x to the nearest integer.
